## Steps

Let's do this one step at a time, mainly for my own sake in implementing and testing the code. We'll start with "Chunking".

### Chunking

Using ["output"]["html_tokens_per_page"] from `config.toml`, divide the text thus:

- Since we're working with *tokenized* editions, we will assume that the natural unit can be identified by dropping the last two elements (`.token1`) from the CTS-URN. **Except for Drama…**
- For drama, the natural unit is complicated: speech, but also line.
- For *prose* and *poetry*, we can chunk by natural unit, aiming for `tokens_per_page`, but preferring to keep natural units together. 
- In a prose text like Herodotus (cited by book/section), we'll chunk by section, unless a section contains fewer than `tokens_per_page`, in which case we'll also grab the next one, even if that exceeds `tokens_per_page`.
- In the case of poetry, likewise. We grab lines until we have `tokens_per_page`, but always including a whole line, even if that means exceeding `tokens_per_page` a little.
- In the case of drama, we always want to keep lines intact. Those will be cited by the first (left-most) component of the citation-element in the CTS-URN, *e.g.* `urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:1`. In our editions, the second element indicates the "speech"; this is useful when a single poetic line involes more than one speaker:

	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:7.1.speaker#Διόνυσος
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:7.1.text#θαρρῶν γε· μόνον ἐκεῖνʼ ὅπως μὴ ʼρεῖς,
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:7.2.speaker#Ξανθίας
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:7.2.text#τὸ τί;

- But we can *also* define "speech" as "many lines in a row from the same speaker":

	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:209.1.speaker#Βάτραχοι
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:209.1.text#βρεκεκεκὲξ κοὰξ κοάξ,
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:210.1.speaker#Βάτραχοι
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:210.1.text#βρεκεκεκὲξ κοὰξ κοάξ.
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:211.1.speaker#Βάτραχοι
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:211.1.text#λιμναῖα κρηνῶν τέκνα,
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:212.1.speaker#Βάτραχοι
	urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:212.1.text#ξύναυλον ὕμνων βοὰν

- So we will chunk by line, but prefer to keep speeches—many lines from the same speaker—intact, even if that means exceeding `tokens_per_page`.

We got this error, but I know just why! The values in the citation-fields in a URN, *e.g.* `urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0019.tlg009.fu.sp:244b.1.text.token1` are *never* numbers, they are just labels (that look like numbers).

The philosophy is this: We get *enumeration* of text-order from the order of citations in a `.cex` file. The URNs just label each line. So those should always be treated as Strings, whether they are `244` or `244b`.

(Those `244b` values are historical, when some editor of manuscripts decided to re-order the poem, or an editor interpolates a line from another source.)
